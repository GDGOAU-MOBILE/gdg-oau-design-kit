# Social posts, flyers and banners

## Sizes

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Instagram / LinkedIn portrait | 1080 × 1350 px |
| Square | 1080 × 1080 px |
| Story / WhatsApp status | 1080 × 1920 px |
| X / LinkedIn landscape | 1600 × 900 px |
| A4 flyer | 210 × 297 mm, 3 mm bleed |
| Roll-up banner | 850 × 2000 mm |

## Structure

1. Pastel background or an official pattern (`assets/brand/pattern-*.png`) cropped behind a white card.
2. GDG mark and "GDG on Campus OAU" top left. Partner logos sit in a row at the bottom, same height.
3. A mono kicker (event type), the headline (Google Sans Bold, one highlighted phrase), and at most two
   lines of supporting text.
4. Facts as pills: date, time, venue.
5. One call to action in a filled pill: "Register at gdgoau.com/…".

## Rules

- One message per post. If you need a third line of text, make a carousel.
- Speaker posts: circular photo in a white ring with an ink outline, name bold, role muted, talk title.
- No stock photos of people who are not part of the event.
- Use #GDGOnCampus on GDGOAU socials.
- Text on images must pass the writing rules too. No emojis on partner-facing posts.
