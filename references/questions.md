# Intake questions

Ask these before you design. Ask in batches of up to four, most important first. Offer a recommended
option where one exists. Skip any question the user has already answered in the conversation, and do not
ask again for something you can find in the files they gave you.

## Always

1. **What are we making?** Slide deck, PDF/proposal, website or page, social post or flyer, banner, other.
2. **Who will read it, and what should they do afterwards?** (For example: a bank's sponsorship team,
   to agree to be Title Partner.)
3. **What are the source materials?** Documents, chats, links, past decks. Which one wins if they disagree?
4. **Format and size?** PPTX and PDF (default for decks), A4 PDF, 16:9, 1080 × 1350 social, web.
5. **Deadline and length limit?** (For example: "max 12 slides, today".)

## For a partner or sponsor pitch

6. **Partner details:** exact company name, the product to feature (if any), and a logo file. If there is
   no logo, ask permission to look for an official one (app store icon, company site).
7. **The ask:** which tier or amount, and whether partial support is welcome.
8. **Any personal link?** For example an alumnus in leadership, or a programme of theirs that fits. Get
   permission before naming a person, and confirm their title and how to address them.
9. **What was promised already?** Anything the team told the partner in calls or chats (for example "every
   attendee will get an account"). Promises must be phrased so they stay voluntary for students.
10. **Constraints the partner mentioned:** sponsorship windows, budget cycles, brand rules.
11. **Who is the contact person on the last slide?** Name, role, email. Phone numbers only with permission.

## For an event

12. Date, time, venue (hall names), expected attendance, the programme, the budget and its split.

## For a website

13. Pages, the main action (register, contact, donate), the stack the repo uses, and where it deploys.

## Before you build, confirm

Read the plan back to the user in one short list (one line per slide, page or section) and wait for a go
ahead when the piece has more than five parts or goes to an external partner.
